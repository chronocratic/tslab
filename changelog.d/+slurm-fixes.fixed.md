Studies on SLURM: a typo in a machine profile names the close key and lists the valid ones. Values in `#SBATCH` lines are quoted, so a study folder or job name with a space works. Job output is unbuffered. A job waits for its tasks file on shared storage. A follow-up job submits without the SLURM variables of its own job. Pending array tasks get their state from `sacct`. A completed job gets a short wait for its `status.json`. Preemption and node failure do not use up the retries of a run. `study wait` stops asking about jobs that ended.
