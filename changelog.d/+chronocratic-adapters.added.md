chronocratic adapters as the extras `chronocratic-models` and `chronocratic-datasets`. Every chronocratic model and datamodule is found automatically and configured from the command line with settings generated from chronocratic's own classes; augmentations are given as recipes that appear only on models of the matching view type; `build_chronocratic_model`, `build_chronocratic_data_source` and `ChronocraticEncoder` connect them to the ready stages; `model_settings` and `data_settings` build settings by name for presets.
