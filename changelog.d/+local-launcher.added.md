Local launches stop cleanly on `Ctrl-C`, `SIGTERM` and `SIGHUP`, take an optional time limit per task, and run several tasks at once: GPUs are found automatically, locked across studies, or replaced by CPU slots. They keep the machine awake, detach with `--detach`, and support `tslab study cancel` and `tslab study usage`. The train stage now records why training stopped in `training.json`, and warns when `max_training_time` stopped it.
