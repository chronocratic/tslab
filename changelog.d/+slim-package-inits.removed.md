Removed the re-exports of internal subpackages: `tslab.lab.cli`, `tslab.lab.cli.common`, `tslab.lab.cli.study`, `tslab.lab.core.meta`, `tslab.lab.study.commands`, `.definition`, `.machine`, `.launchers.local`, `.launchers.slurm`, `tslab.ml.interfaces` and `tslab.ts.interfaces` no longer export names, and a few helpers left the doors of `tslab.lab.analysis`, `tslab.lab.jobs.slurm`, `tslab.ml.lightning` and `tslab.ts.tasks`. Import from `tslab.lab`, `tslab.ml` or `tslab.ts`, or from the module that defines the name. The stages are imported only through `tslab.ml.stages` and `tslab.ts.stages`: `tslab.ml.train` and `tslab.ts.encode`, `evaluate`, `check_representations` and `ModelAsEncoder` are gone. See the migration page.
