The typed converter moved from `tslab.core.config` to `tslab.core.codec`: `config_to_data`, `decode_dataclass`, `strip_annotated`, `union_choices` and `union_members` are now imported from `tslab.core.codec`. `tslab.core.config` keeps `save_config`, `load_config`, `parse_config`, `config_to_yaml`, `config_from_yaml`, `ConfigFileError` and `REQUIRED`; the public API of `tslab` is unchanged.
